Dreaming you can't run, move or scream

Legs that feel like lead, a scream that makes no sound, a phone you can't dial. These dreams of being unable to act are frustrating and very common.

Common themes

  • Feeling powerless. Many people link these dreams to a situation where they feel they can't change the outcome.
  • Not being heard. A silent scream can mirror feeling ignored or unable to speak up.
  • The sleeping body. During REM sleep the body is naturally paralysed so we don't act out dreams. The dream may weave this real sensation into the story.
  • Hesitation. Being stuck can echo holding back from a step you know you need to take.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Where in life do you feel unable to act?
  • Who did you want to call or warn?
  • Have you been holding your voice back lately?
