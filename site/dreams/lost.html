Dreaming of being lost

Wandering through streets you don't recognise, corridors that loop back, or a forest with no path: being lost is a common dream and often a frustrating one.

Common themes

  • Lack of direction. Being lost can mirror uncertainty about where life, work or a relationship is heading.
  • A big decision. When you are torn between options, the mind can turn it into a maze.
  • New territory. A new job, city or stage of life can feel like streets with no map.
  • Looking for something. Notice what or who you were searching for. It may be what you need.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • What were you trying to find?
  • Is there a decision you are stuck on?
  • Did you find your way in the end?
