Dreaming your partner is cheating

Waking up from a dream that your partner cheated can leave you upset with them, even though they did nothing. These dreams are common and, on their own, are not evidence of anything.

Common themes

  • Insecurity. The dream may reflect fear of losing someone, or feeling less important to them.
  • Feeling left out. If your partner has been busy or distant, the dream may express feeling sidelined, by work, family or a hobby.
  • Past hurt. Old betrayals, yours or someone else's, can surface in a new relationship.
  • Your own wishes. Sometimes it is less about the partner and more about a part of life you want more of.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Have you felt distant from your partner lately?
  • Who was the other person, and what do they represent to you?
  • Is an old hurt resurfacing?
