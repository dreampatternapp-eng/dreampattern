Dreaming of fire

Fire in dreams can be destructive, like a burning house or a wildfire, or warm, like a fireplace or candle. Its meaning often depends on whether it was under control.

Common themes

  • Strong emotion. Fire can mirror anger, passion or frustration that feels hard to contain.
  • Destruction and renewal. Something burning down can feel like loss, but fire also clears the way for new growth.
  • Urgency. An emergency can reflect a problem you feel has to be dealt with now.
  • Warmth. A fire under control can stand for comfort, connection and energy.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Was the fire under control or spreading?
  • What was burning?
  • Is there anger or passion you have been keeping in?
