Dreaming of dogs

Dogs appear often in dreams, as beloved pets, strays, or occasionally as a threat. The meaning tends to depend on how the dog behaved and what dogs mean to you.

Common themes

  • Loyalty and friendship. A friendly dog can reflect trust, companionship, or someone who has your back.
  • Protection. A guard dog may point to boundaries, being protected, or wanting to protect someone.
  • Conflict. A dog that bites or attacks can mirror tension with someone close, or a betrayal you fear.
  • A pet you have lost. Dreaming of a pet who has died is a common and often comforting way of remembering them.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Was the dog friendly, scared or aggressive?
  • Did you know the dog?
  • Who in your life feels loyal, or not, right now?
