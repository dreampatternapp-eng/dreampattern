Dreaming of being late or missing a flight

You are rushing for a flight, a train or an important event, and every obstacle conspires to make you late. Bags won't close, roads loop back, the clock jumps forward. These dreams are frustrating and very common.

Common themes

  • Missed opportunities. The dream may echo a worry that a chance is slipping past, or that you are behind where you think you should be.
  • Overcommitment. When there is too much to do, the mind can turn it into a race you cannot win.
  • Transitions. Planes, trains and journeys often show up around life changes. Missing one can mirror hesitation about moving on.
  • Perfectionism. If you are usually punctual and organised, the dream may play out the very thing you work hardest to avoid.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Where were you trying to go, and does it remind you of anything?
  • Do you feel behind in some part of life right now?
  • What kept getting in the way?
