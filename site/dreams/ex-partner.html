Dreaming about your ex

Dreaming about an ex is very common, including for people who are happy in a new relationship and have no wish to go back. The dream is often less about the person than about what they stood for.

Common themes

  • Unfinished feelings. Things left unsaid, or a breakup that never quite made sense, can resurface in dreams.
  • A pattern repeating. An ex can appear when a current situation echoes an old dynamic, in love, friendship or work.
  • A past version of you. The dream may be about who you were in that time of life, more than the other person.
  • Comparison. New relationships can stir memories of old ones. That is normal and does not mean something is wrong.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • What was happening in the dream between you?
  • Does anything in your life right now feel like that relationship did?
  • What do you miss, or not miss, about who you were then?
