Dreaming of flying

Flying dreams are often some of the most joyful dreams people remember. You might soar over cities, glide just above the ground, or struggle to stay up. Many lucid dreamers say flying is the first thing they try once they realise they are dreaming.

Common themes

  • Freedom. Rising above everything can reflect a sense of release, or a wish for more of it.
  • Confidence and perspective. Seeing the world from above can echo a moment where things finally make sense or feel within reach.
  • Escape. Sometimes flying is about getting away from something below. Notice what you were leaving behind.
  • Struggling to stay up. Flying that keeps failing, or needs huge effort, can mirror goals that feel close but hard to sustain.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Was flying easy, or did you have to work to stay in the air?
  • Where were you going, or what were you leaving?
  • Did you realise you were dreaming while it happened?
