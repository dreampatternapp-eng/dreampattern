Dreaming of a loved one who has died

Dreams of someone who has died are among the most meaningful dreams people describe. They can feel comforting, sad, ordinary, or startlingly real.

Common themes

  • Grief. Dreams are one of the ways the mind processes loss, sometimes for years afterwards.
  • Comfort and connection. Many people wake feeling they had a visit, a chance to see someone again or say something left unsaid.
  • Guidance. The person may appear when you face a choice they would have had an opinion on.
  • Anniversaries. These dreams often cluster around birthdays, holidays and the date of the loss.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • What did they say or do in the dream?
  • How did you feel when you woke up?
  • Is there something you would like to tell them?
