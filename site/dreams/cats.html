Dreaming of cats

Cats in dreams can feel affectionate, aloof or mysterious. Many people associate them with independence, intuition and a certain wildness.

Common themes

  • Independence. A cat can stand for doing things your own way, or a wish for more freedom.
  • Intuition. Cats are often linked to instinct and quiet awareness, a reminder to trust your gut.
  • Comfort. A cat curled up with you can mirror a need for rest, warmth or self-care.
  • Distrust. A hissing or scratching cat may reflect someone you are unsure about, or feeling unwelcome.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • How did the cat treat you?
  • Is your intuition telling you something you have been ignoring?
  • Do you need more independence or more comfort right now?
