Dreaming of a wedding

Wedding dreams can be joyful, chaotic or surreal: marrying a stranger, forgetting the dress, or arriving at the wrong ceremony. They often appear around commitments of any kind.

Common themes

  • Commitment. A wedding can mirror a promise you are making, to a person, a job or a plan.
  • Union. Bringing two things together, such as two parts of your life or two ideas, can show up as a marriage.
  • Pressure and expectations. A wedding going wrong can echo worry about meeting other people's expectations.
  • Real plans. If a wedding is coming up, these dreams are a normal way of processing the excitement and stress.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Who were you marrying, if anyone?
  • What commitment is on your mind?
  • Did the day go to plan?
