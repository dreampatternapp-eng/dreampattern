Dreaming of a car out of control or brakes failing

Brakes that don't work, a steering wheel that won't turn, or sitting in the back seat with nobody driving: car dreams often capture a very specific kind of panic.

Common themes

  • Control and direction. Cars carry us through life. A car out of control can mirror feeling that life's direction is not in your hands.
  • Who is driving. If someone else was at the wheel, consider whether another person has too much say in your choices.
  • Moving too fast. Failing brakes can echo a pace you cannot slow down, such as a busy schedule or a fast-moving situation.
  • Responsibility. Crashing or nearly crashing may reflect a fear of making a costly mistake.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Who was driving the car?
  • Where were you heading, and did you get there?
  • Is there something in life moving faster than you would like?
