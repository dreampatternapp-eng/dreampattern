Dreaming of a tsunami or giant wave

A huge wave rising on the horizon, and the dread of knowing it is coming, is a memorable dream. Some people run, some watch, and some are swept up.

Common themes

  • Overwhelm. Like drowning dreams, a tsunami can mirror stress that feels like it will wash everything away.
  • Change you can see coming. A wave in the distance can stand for a change you know is on its way, such as a move, a birth or a deadline.
  • Emotional release. Big waves can reflect feelings that have built up for a long time.
  • Surviving it. Many people find the wave passes or they float. That can be reassuring to notice.

Dreams are personal, and no symbol has one fixed meaning. Treat these as starting points for your own reflection, not predictions.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Did you see the wave coming?
  • What big change is on your horizon?
  • Were you alone, or with other people?
