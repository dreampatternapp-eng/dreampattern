Speak it before it fades

Tap once when you wake and tell DreamPattern what you saw. It turns your voice into a journal entry, on your phone where possible.

A thoughtful reading

Get a reflective interpretation of symbols, emotions and themes, written to help you think, not to tell you what your dream "means".

See what keeps coming back

DreamPattern tracks recurring people, places and symbols over time, so you can notice the patterns in your dream life.

Private by design

No account. Your journal lives on your phone, can be locked with Face ID, and is never used for ads or tracking.
