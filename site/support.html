Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DreamPattern. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings.

I reinstalled or got a new phone. Where's my Premium?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases.

Where are my dreams stored? Can I move them to a new phone?

Your journal is stored only on your phone, and we have no copy. Before switching phones, use Settings → Export as text (or PDF with Premium) to save your dreams. Deleting the app deletes your journal.

Why didn't voice entry work?

Make sure DreamPattern has microphone and speech recognition permission in your phone's Settings. Some phones need an internet connection for speech-to-text.

How many free readings do I get?

Three AI readings per calendar month. Premium removes the monthly limit.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data erases everything on your phone. See our Privacy Policy for what our server keeps and how to ask us to delete it.
