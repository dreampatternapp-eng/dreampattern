## Description (4000)

Dreams fade within minutes of waking. DreamPattern helps you catch them, understand them, and see what keeps coming back.

SPEAK IT BEFORE IT FADES
Tap once and tell DreamPattern what you saw. Your words become a journal entry, transcribed on your phone where supported. Prefer typing? That works too. Add a mood, mark lucid dreams, and tag people, places and feelings.

A THOUGHTFUL READING
Ask for an AI reading and get a reflective look at the symbols, emotions and themes in your dream. Readings are written to help you think, not to tell you what your dream "means", and they never claim to predict the future.

FIND YOUR PATTERNS
DreamPattern keeps track of the symbols, people, places and feelings that recur in your dreams, shows which ones are rising or fading, and connects new dreams to your history.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• Lock it with Face ID or your passcode
• No ads, no tracking, no data sales

• Share a dream as an image

## App Privacy (nutrition label) answers

Tracking: **No**, we do not track users across apps or websites.

Data collected (all: purpose **App Functionality** only, **not linked** to the user's identity, **not used for tracking**):

| Apple category | Data type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| User Content | Other User Content | Dream text sent for an AI reading (passed to Anthropic, not stored by us) |
| Identifiers | User ID | Anonymous app ID used to count free readings and check Premium |
| Purchases | Purchase History | RevenueCat checks subscription status |

Not collected: contact info, health, location, contacts, browsing, search, diagnostics, usage data, audio (speech is converted by the phone's speech service, never sent to us).

Note: the dream text is processed in real time and not stored by our server, but Anthropic may retain API data for a limited period under its policy, so we declare it rather than claim "not collected". This is the conservative, honest choice.
